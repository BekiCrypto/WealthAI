## Direction contract

THESIS: WealthAI is a live storm-warning center for markets: every asset is
a tracked system, every economic release is an advisory, and confidence is
a forecast-cone width, not a bare percentage. Refuses the category-default
"dark fintech dashboard" -- this reads as a meteorological situation board,
not a trading-app skin.

OWN-WORLD: Deep storm-slate ground (cold blue-grey near-black, not
navy-purple fintech default). Committed strategy: one saturated pressure-teal
carries 40-50% of the surface as structural accent (borders, chart lines,
headers), never scattered. Functional colors render as advisory-severity
colors: bullish = all-clear teal-green, bearish = warning red-orange,
hawkish = advisory amber, dovish = the pressure-teal family, and a single
saturated crimson is reserved for the most severe category only (shock-level
geopolitical risk), giving real color-coded severity tiers instead of flat
red/green. Type: Space Grotesk (bold/condensed display -- its scientific-
instrument heritage fits a forecast console) for headlines, Space Mono
(teletype-bulletin heritage) for every instrument readout and advisory
bulletin body, Plus Jakarta Sans for UI/body prose. Components: dated
"advisory bulletin" cards with a monospace body; a real forecast-cone SVG
(narrow at now, widening into uncertainty, colored by severity) replacing
the flat outcome-band strip; tracked-system cards (category badge + mini
trajectory sparkline + cone preview) replacing plain score cards; a circular
pressure-gauge dial for Intelligence Score confidence instead of a bare tag.

STORY: A user reads the day like a forecast-desk meteorologist: which
systems (assets) are intensifying, what's the current global pressure (risk
regime), which advisories (events) are active or upcoming, and for any
system, its forecast cone and recommended track (trade setup). The Learning
Center is the "Forecaster's Handbook": certification levels (Trainee
Observer -> Certified Forecaster -> Senior Meteorologist -> Chief of Watch),
quizzes as certification exams, streaks as consecutive watch days,
achievements as service ribbons.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard opens as a live Situation Board: a header advisory
ticker stating the active risk regime as a headline in its severity color
("RISK-OFF ADVISORY IN EFFECT"), a grid of tracked systems each a compact
forecast card (category badge + cone sparkline), and the day's advisory
schedule (upcoming events) as a bulletin list. Primary action: open any
system's full forecast (asset detail page).

FORM: Storm Warning, grounded candidate #1 of 7 (ordered: storm-tracking,
seismograph, FT/Economist editorial, ship's chart table, central-bank
policy statement, ATC radar, trading-pit badges), presented as IMPECCABLE'S
PICK and chosen by the user over the dice-assigned direction (Ship's Chart
Table / celestial navigation, seed key df7e0692, assigned index 4). Raised
by donations from the dealt challengers per that round's verdicts: Cyclorama
Dawn (competitive) donates its time-phase/horizon-band discipline into a
session-of-day atmosphere layered on the Situation Board; Zoo/Gardens Map
(declined) donates flat unmodulated advisory-severity color fields (no
gradients) and capsule-chip controls with a live-position indicator chip;
Phosphor Terminal (declined) donates tabular monospace confidence for every
instrument readout, carried in Space Mono; Timetable Slide Rack
(competitive) donates its single-size/rank-by-weight-and-case discipline
into the advisory bulletin body text, which never resizes for hierarchy --
matching real NOAA teletype bulletins; Variable Font Specimen (declined)
donates a brief live digit-roll on any instrument readout when its value
refreshes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying
its provenance.
